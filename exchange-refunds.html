01

Exchanges

You can exchange a pair for another size or another model within 14 days of delivery. The pair must be unworn, with its tags, in its original box and with the box undamaged.

To start an exchange, message us on WhatsApp or email with your order number. We arrange the pickup; the courier fee for exchanges is LE 60 inside Cairo and Giza and LE 90 elsewhere.

02

Refunds

If the pair you received is faulty or not what you ordered, we refund the full amount including shipping. For other returns we refund the price of the pair once it reaches us and passes inspection.

Refunds for cash on delivery orders are paid by bank transfer, InstaPay or mobile wallet within 7 working days.

03

What we cannot accept

Worn pairs, pairs without their original box, and items bought on sale or marked final sale.

04

Checking your order

You can open the parcel and check your pair in front of the courier before you pay. If something is wrong, refuse the delivery and contact us.

Questions?

Message us on WhatsApp or email hello@mksneakers.com.
